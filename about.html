Technical Product Manager

ALL-LLLM POD started as a simple idea: combine my interests in NLP, generative AI, and comedy into a single project. What began as an experiment in persona simulation quickly became a vehicle for understanding how modern AI systems are built, operated, evaluated, and debugged.

The tooling became the more interesting story. Transcript generation, speaker attribution, and HITL review workflows matter because frontier AI companies have already exhausted much of the low-hanging fruit for model training: freely available text has been scraped from the internet repeatedly. The next meaningful gains will almost certainly come from transforming audio and video into usable training data, and that process is far from trivial.
