Technical Product Manager

My career in tech has been spent building systems that sit between people, data, and software — mostly integrations and workflow automation in HR, benefits, and SaaS.

ALL-LLLM POD started as a simple idea: combine my interests in NLP, generative AI, and comedy into a single project. What began as an experiment in persona simulation quickly became a vehicle for understanding how modern AI systems are built, operated, evaluated, and debugged.

Over time, the project grew into a production-grade platform spanning transcript ingestion, speaker attribution, HITL review workflows, distributed inference, persona memory systems, and forensic tooling for investigating model failures. What began as curiosity eventually became infrastructure.
