Technical Product Manager - I solve problems

My career has largely been spent building systems that sit between people, data, and software. At Zenefits, Ease, and Blissfully, that meant integrations, workflow automation, operational tooling, and data infrastructure. The domains changed, but the underlying problems were often the same: fragmented systems, inconsistent data, unclear ownership, and processes that broke down at scale.

ALL-LLLM POD became an opportunity to explore those same challenges through a different lens. What started as an experiment in AI personas quickly evolved into a series of questions about training data quality, observability, evaluation, and human oversight. Building convincing AI personalities turned out to be less about prompting models and more about constructing reliable systems capable of producing trustworthy inputs.

Along the way I built transcript processing pipelines, speaker attribution systems, human-in-the-loop review workflows, distributed inference infrastructure, multimodal iOS applications, and forensic tooling for diagnosing model failures. Many of the most valuable lessons came from investigating why systems failed rather than celebrating when they worked.

The project reflects how I approach product problems. I enjoy operating where product strategy, technical systems, and operational reality intersect. My instinct is to follow problems to their root cause, build visibility into the system, and create tools that allow both humans and software to perform better together.

The visible outcome is a collection of AI-generated podcast personalities. The more interesting outcome is the infrastructure behind them: transcript pipelines, evaluation tooling, observability systems, operator workflows, and the pursuit of trustworthy training data. While the premise is intentionally playful, the underlying work is focused on reliability, quality, and understanding how complex AI systems succeed or fail.
