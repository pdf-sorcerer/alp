Observability · Attribution Tokens · Push Notifications

Resume Intelligence - from Slopeumé.dev

2026-07-03

I'm job hunting and things are in "hurry up and wait" mode heading into the weekend. In years past, what helped keep the momentum of the search going was the occasional LinkedIn notification: "Soandso has viewed your profile."

Today, most of my LinkedIn notifications are people in my network playing various LinkedIn Games. It reminds me of Star Trek: The Next Generation's S5E6, "The Game" , where Ashley Judd and Wil Wheaton save the day. Anyway... between that, the rise in anonymous profile viewers, and whatever's happening in the LinkedIn firmament, I can't help wondering whether my resume is even making it in front of a recruiter.

The Thought
Question: Are my applications being looked at by recruiters?

Answer: Let's find out!

I added custom attribution tokens to Slopeumé.dev, a job search management tool I made a couple months ago. Every resume I generate in-app contains a custom URL to my portfolio and LinkedIn profile.

If my resume makes it to a recruiter or anyone else unaltered, and they click my LinkedIn or portfolio links, those resources load normally for the viewer. Behind the scenes, a lightweight redirect service built with Vercel and Supabase records the click before the event arrives on my phone as a push notification with a library of Love Island 🔊 "I got a text/message!" recordings played round-robin. 

+-------------------+-----------+--------------------------------------+
| STAGE             | TOOL      | FUNCTION                             |
+-------------------+-----------+--------------------------------------+
| Resume Generation | Slopume   | Creates application-specific links   |
| Link Routing      | Vercel    | Captures click before redirect       |
| Event Logging     | Supabase  | Records company, target, timestamp   |
| Notification      | iOS App   | Banner + Love Island audio           |
