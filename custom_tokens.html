Mason: Is resume actually making it in front of a human? Can I test that?

Yup, I can test that.

Every resume I generate in Slopumé.dev—a job aggregator and application management tool I built a couple of months ago— contains unique, application-specific attribution tokens embedded in my Portfolio and LinkedIn links.

BT dubs: Portfolio • LinkedIn

If my resume makes it to a recruiter or anyone else unaltered, and they click one of those links, the destination loads normally. Behind the scenes, a lightweight redirect service built with Vercel and Supabase records the event and sends a push notification to my phone identifying which application generated the click. The goal isn't to change the hiring process—it's simply to restore a small measure of observability to an otherwise opaque one. 

+-------------------+-----------+--------------------------------------+
| STAGE             | TOOL      | FUNCTION                             |
+-------------------+-----------+--------------------------------------+
| Resume Generation | Slopume   | Creates application-specific links   |
| Link Routing      | Vercel    | Captures click before redirect       |
| Event Logging     | Supabase  | Records company, target, timestamp   |
| Notification      | iOS App   | Banner push notification             |
