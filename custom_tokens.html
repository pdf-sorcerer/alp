Job Search Observability · Attribution Tokens · iOS Notifications

Realtime Resume Intel - from Slopeumé.dev

I'm job hunting at the moment and things are in 'hurry up and wait' mode. One small thing that always perked me up in times past was LinkedIn's little notification: "Someone viewed your profile."

Thees ays, it's people in my network playing various LinkedIn games (like, wtf?).

Is this my lack of profile activity a reflection of the market? Are recruiters struggling with application volume? The alignment of Mars and the Moon over the pyramids? Probably all of the above.

At the end of the day: is my resume even making it in front of a human?

The Question
Question: Are my applications even being looked at in a tough market where recruiters are buried with volume?

Answer: Not sure. Let's try to find out.

Tuesday night I added custom tokens to Slopume, my job search management tool. Every resume I generate in-app contains a customized link to my portfolio and LinkedIn profile.

If my resume makes it to a human, when they click on my LinkedIn or Portfolio links - those resources load for the viewer, and lightweight redirect service built with Vercel and Supabase before arriving on my phone as a push notification, complete with one of two dozen variations of the Love Island "I've got a text!" sound, played round-robin because gotta keep things fresh.

I know what my technical audience is thinking: what if ATS platforms strip customized URLs? I agree, not sure at time of writing if that happens. Worth looking into and thinking about possible ways of hardening the process. Even so, this won't catch every interaction—but it'll catch some, and that's enough to create signal.

As an unintended happy side effect, it also partially de-ambiguizes LinkedIn's anonymous profile viewers. I don't really care who viewed my profile. I care which company they work for.
