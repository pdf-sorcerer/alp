Job Search Observability · Attribution Tokens · iOS Notifications

Resume Intelligence - from Slopeumé.dev

I'm job hunting at the moment, and things are in "hurry up and wait" mode. In the past, a neat distraction that kept the ball rolling was the occasional notification informing me that, "Soandso has viewed your profile."

These days, most of my LinkedIn notifications inform me that people in my network are playing various LinkedIn Games. It has been disorienting. And it makes me wonder whether the silence is just the LinkedIn algorithm, the job market... or whether my resume is even making it in front of a recruiter.

The Thought
Question: Are my applications being looked at by recruiters?

Answer: Let's find out!

I added custom attribution tokens to Slopeumé.dev, a job search management tool I made a couple months ago. Every resume I generate in-app contains a custom URL to my portfolio and LinkedIn profile.

Hypothetically, if my resume makes it to a recruiter or anyone else unaltered, and they click my LinkedIn or portfolio links, those resources load normally for the viewer. Behind the scenes, a lightweight redirect service built with Vercel and Supabase records the click before the event arrives on my phone as a push notification with a library of Love Island 🔊 "I got a text/message!" recordings played round-robin.

Now, my technical audience is probably thinking: what if talent platforms strip customized URLs? I agree. I'm not sure whether that happens. Worth Googling and thinking about ways of hardening the design. Even so, the system won't catch every interaction, but it will catch some, and that's enough to create signal.

Oh also, this partially disambiguates LinkedIn's anonymous profile viewers. Ultimately, I shouldn't really care who viewed my profile. I care which company they work for.
